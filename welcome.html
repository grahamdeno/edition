A feed that ends.

Social feeds are built so you never finish. Yours ends. A few things come back each day; clear them and close the app.

Your reason
comes back with it.

Every post starts with why you kept it, in your own words. If you didn't say when you saved it, Shoebox asks when it comes back.

  • Your words on topThe line you wrote when you saved it leads the post, above the link or the photo.
  • Three choicesDone, Later or Let go. Nothing else to decide.
  • Later, on a day you pickTomorrow, this weekend or a date you choose. It comes back that morning.
  • Nothing goes by accidentSomething you keep putting off fades after a few weeks, then waits for your answer before it goes.

What's in the box

Save

From anywhere

Share a link, a photo or a video from any app, or type a note. Add why you're keeping it.

Later

Bring it back on a date

Set something aside until tomorrow, the weekend or a date you pick. It comes back that morning with a reminder.

Find

Everything you kept

Still to come, finished or let go. One search covers every note, link and photo.

Read

It does the reading

In the iPhone app, each save gets a title, a two-line summary and tags, so you know what it was before you open it.

Glance

On your Home Screen

The iPhone widget shows how many are left today. Tap one to open it.

Keep

Backed up every day

The iPhone app saves a copy to Files and iCloud Drive each morning. Moving to a new phone takes one tap.

It lives on your phone.

No account

Nothing to sign up for. Your saves stay on your device.

No ads, no tracking

Nothing is sold, and nothing follows you around the web.

Yours to delete

One button erases it all, here and on our server. Read the privacy policy.
