What's in the box

Save

From anywhere

Share a link, a photo or a video from any app, or type a note. Add why you're keeping it.

Later

Bring it back on a date

Set something aside until the weekend or next month. It comes back that morning with a reminder.

Find

Everything you kept

Loved, finished, still to read. One search covers every note, link and photo.

Read

It does the reading

Each save gets a title, a two-line summary and tags, so you know what it was before you open it.

Look

Live weather cams

A live camera near you, for the beach, the mountain or the traffic, right under the forecast.

Keep

Backed up every day

The iPhone app saves a copy to Files and iCloud Drive each morning. Moving to a new phone takes one tap.

It lives on your phone.

No account

Nothing to sign up for. Your saves stay on your device.

No ads, no tracking

Nothing is sold, and nothing follows you around the web.

Yours to delete

One button erases it all, here and on our server. Read the privacy policy.
