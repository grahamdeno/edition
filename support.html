Shoebox

Support

Shoebox is a calm daily feed of the things you save, handed back a few at a time each morning.

Get help

Email support@tangomike.app. Starting the subject with [Shoebox] gets it to the right place. From the app, Settings › Contact support fills that in for you, with the app version.

Common questions

Where is my data? On your phone. Shoebox has no accounts. Use Settings › Data › Export backup to keep a copy or move to a new phone.

Do I need an AI key? No. AI is built in. You can turn it off in Settings › AI, and Shoebox still works.

What's the difference between Done and Let go? Done means you finished it and it shapes what Shoebox suggests. Let go means you no longer want it; it leaves your feed.

Can I get something back later? Yes. Set it aside until a date. It comes back that morning with a reminder.
