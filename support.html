Support

Questions, problems or ideas: a person reads every message.

Email support@inmyshoebox.com. In the app, Settings › Help and feedback › Email support starts the message for you, with your app version filled in.

Common questions

Where is my data?

On your phone. Shoebox has no accounts. Settings › Your data › Backup saves a copy, and Import backup moves everything to a new phone. The iPhone app also backs up to Files and iCloud Drive every day.

Do I need an AI key?

No. AI is built into the iPhone app. You can turn it off in Settings › AI, and Shoebox still works.

Why is AI off in my browser?

Shoebox pays for its AI, and anyone can open the web version, so AI comes with the iPhone app, which can prove it's the real Shoebox. In the browser, saving, your feed, search, email in and reminders all still work. Ask for the iPhone beta.

What's my Shoebox email address for?

Newsletters, school email and anything you forward. Find it in Settings › Email to Shoebox. Mail sent there shows up as posts, with its flyers and PDFs read too.

What's the difference between Done and Let go?

Done means you finished it, and it shapes what Shoebox suggests. Let go means you no longer want it, and it leaves your feed.

Can I get something back later?

Yes. Tap Later on a card and pick when. It comes back that morning with a reminder.

How do I delete everything?

Settings › Your data › Privacy › Delete all my data. It removes your saves and settings from your phone, and your email address, waiting mail and AI sign-up from Shoebox's server.
