Privacy

Effective October 27, 2026 (updated October 29, 2026: Shoebox no longer uses your location or gets weather, and no longer finds a quote of the day)

Shoebox keeps what you save on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking across apps or websites, and nothing is sold.

What stays on your phone

Your saved links, notes, photos, files, tags, settings and reading history are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. Backups you export are files you control.

What leaves your phone, and why

Shoebox does not ask for or use your location.

  • AI (iPhone app, only if you turn it on). To read, tag and rank what you save, find pictures and turn newsletters into posts, Shoebox sends the text of the item, your notes on it and any photo you add to Anthropic's Claude, through Shoebox's server. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models. For the one-line summary at the top of Today, AI sees your school post headlines (with your child's name left out) and how many calendar events are left and when the next starts, never their titles. Shoebox's server passes it through and does not keep it. The server keeps a random install number, a fingerprint of its access code, Apple's confirmation that it's the real Shoebox app (App Attest, which identifies no person) and a daily count of AI use, to keep costs in check.
  • Link previews. When you save a link, Shoebox's server fetches that page to read its title, summary and pictures. The page address is not kept.
  • Calendar. In the iPhone app, your calendars are read on your phone and never leave it. If you add a calendar address instead, it passes through Shoebox's server to be read. It is not kept.
  • Beta requests. If you ask to join the iPhone beta, your name, email and answers are kept on Shoebox's server for 90 days so we can decide on invites. Your answers, without your name or email, are read by Anthropic's Claude to suggest whether Shoebox is a good fit; a person makes the decision.
  • Reminders. If you turn on notifications, the reminder's title and time, and your device's notification address, are kept on Shoebox's server until the reminder is sent.
  • Email in. If you use your Shoebox email address, mail sent to it, with any PDFs and pictures attached, is held on Shoebox's server for up to 30 days, until the app collects it, then deleted.
  • Anonymous usage stats (you can turn them off). Once a day, Shoebox sends counts: how many things were saved, how many days were cleared, which kinds of problem came up. They come with a random number used only for this and the day you started, never with anything you saved, an error's details, your name or your device. Settings › Help › Anonymous usage stats turns this off.
  • Music. Song of the day uses Apple Music on your device. Nothing about your library leaves your phone.

Children

Shoebox is not directed to children under 13. If you turn newsletters into posts and add a child's name, the name stays on your phone: in the newsletter's text AI only sees "your child", and Shoebox puts the name back on the post. Flyers and PDFs attached to a newsletter email are read by AI as they are.

Your choices

Turn AI, notifications, calendar and email in on or off at any time in Settings. To delete everything, use Settings › Privacy › Delete all my data: it removes your saves and settings from the device, and from Shoebox's server your email address, any mail waiting there and your AI sign-up. Backups you exported stay where you put them. You can also email support@inmyshoebox.com to ask for deletion.

Changes
