Privacy

Effective November 1, 2026. This covers Shoebox 2 for iPhone. Shoebox 1, the earlier web version, is covered by the earlier policy.

What you keep stays on your phone. There's no account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, and nothing is sold. A few things pass through Shoebox's server to work, and they're listed here.

What stays on your phone

Your saves, your reasons, notes, photos, files, dates and settings are stored on your iPhone. The smart parts run on the phone too: Apple Intelligence writes the short post for a forwarded email and picks pictures, Apple's Vision finds the subject of a photo, and dates are read by the app itself.

Once a day Shoebox saves a copy to the Files app and, if you use it, your iCloud Drive, which Apple's terms cover. Deleting the app deletes your saves from the phone; copies in Files and iCloud Drive stay until you delete them.

What leaves your phone, and why

Shoebox's server is shoebox-api.tangomike.app, run on Cloudflare.

  • Link previews. When you keep a link, Shoebox's server fetches that page to read its title, summary, text and picture, and sends them to your phone. The address isn't kept. Your phone then downloads the page's picture from that website, which sees your phone's internet address as any visit would.
  • Pictures for posts without one (on unless you turn it off in Settings › Pictures). Your phone writes two to four plain words for the post, never names or what you wrote, and posts about health, money or legal matters are never looked up. Those words go through Shoebox's server to Pixabay to find a photo and aren't kept. Your phone downloads the photo from Pixabay, which can see your phone's internet address but nothing you saved. The photo's credit is shown on the post.
  • Your Shoebox email (only if you set it up). Mail sent to your address, with any attachments, is held on Shoebox's server for up to 30 days until the app collects it, then deleted there. Your address and a private key that ties it to your phone are kept while you use it. On your phone Shoebox keeps a short post written from the email and its first useful link; the email itself isn't kept.
  • Beta requests. If you ask to join the beta, your name, email and answers are kept on Shoebox's server for 90 days to decide on invites, then deleted. Your answers, without your name or email, are read by Anthropic's Claude to suggest whether Shoebox is a good fit; a person makes the decision. Anthropic doesn't use them to train its models.

What Shoebox doesn't do

No location, contacts or calendars. No usage analytics or advertising identifiers. Notifications are scheduled on your phone; nothing about them is sent anywhere. If you choose to share crash reports with developers in iOS Settings, Apple sends them; they don't include your saves.

Children

Shoebox isn't directed to children under 13.

Your choices

  • Turn off pictures for posts without one in Settings › Pictures.
  • Don't set up a Shoebox email, and nothing is held for you on the server.
  • Settings › Delete everything saves one last copy to Files, removes every save from your phone, and asks Shoebox's server to delete your email address and any mail waiting there.
  • Email support@inmyshoebox.com to ask what's held for you, or to have it deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
