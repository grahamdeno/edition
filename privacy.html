Shoebox

Privacy

Effective October 27, 2026

Shoebox keeps what you save on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking across apps or websites, and nothing is sold.

What stays on your phone

Your saved links, notes, photos, files, tags, settings and reading history are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. Backups you export are files you control.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • AI (only if you turn it on). To read, tag and rank what you save, find pictures and turn newsletters into posts, Shoebox sends the text of the item, your notes on it and any photo you add to Anthropic's Claude, through Shoebox's server. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models. Shoebox's server passes it through and does not keep it. The server keeps a random install number, a fingerprint of its access code and a daily count of AI use, to keep costs in check.
  • Link previews. When you save a link, Shoebox's server fetches that page to read its title, summary and pictures. The page address is not kept.
  • Calendar. If you add a calendar, its private address passes through Shoebox's server to be read. It is not kept.
  • Reminders. If you turn on notifications, the reminder's title and time, and your device's notification address, are kept on Shoebox's server until the reminder is sent.
  • Email in. If you use your Shoebox email address, mail sent to it is held on Shoebox's server for up to 30 days, until the app collects it, then deleted.
  • Weather and music. Weather comes from Apple Weather (or Open-Meteo as a backup) and tides from NOAA, for the place you choose; only that place is sent. Song of the day uses Apple Music on your device.

Children

Shoebox is not directed to children under 13. If you turn newsletters into posts and add a child's name, the name stays on your phone: AI only sees "your child", and Shoebox puts the name back on the post.

Your choices

Turn AI, notifications, calendar and email in on or off at any time in Settings. To have anything held on Shoebox's server deleted, email support@tangomike.app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
