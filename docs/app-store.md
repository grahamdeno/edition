**Description**:
Keep it in my shoebox.

You save things everywhere: a recipe texted to yourself, an article in an open tab, a starred school email, a screenshot, a note. You meant to come back to them. Shoebox is where they come back.

YOUR OWN FEED
Everything in it is something you chose. Share a link, photo or video from any app, type a note, or forward an email. Add why you're keeping it, and that line comes back at the top of the post.

A FEED THAT ENDS
A few things come back each day. Each one gets Done, Later or Let go. When you've cleared today, there's nothing more until tomorrow.

FORWARD IT, SHOEBOX READS IT
Your Shoebox has its own email address. Sign up for newsletters with it or add it to your school's parent app. Shoebox reads the email, its flyers and PDFs, and keeps what's for you: dates, forms and fees become posts with a Done button.

LATER, ON A DAY YOU PICK
Set something aside until tomorrow, the weekend or a date you choose. It comes back that morning with a reminder.

IT LIVES ON YOUR PHONE
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your saves stay on your iPhone and are backed up to Files and iCloud Drive each day. One button deletes everything.
