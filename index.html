How it works

Three moves.
That's the whole app.

Shoebox reads the date it is about and brings it back before the day, one post at a time. Clear them and close the app.

Tap it

Keep it

From anywhere,
in a few seconds.

  • Share from any appTap Share, then Shoebox. Add a line about why you're keeping it.
  • Or keep it herePaste a link, write a note, or add a photo, a file or a picture from the camera.
  • Or forward an emailYour Shoebox has its own address. Each email becomes one short post with only what matters, written on your phone. The email itself isn't kept.

It comes back

When it matters,
not when you remember.

Shoebox reads every save for the day it's about: an event, a deadline, a time. Then it brings it back before that day, on its own.
